# OPPO phone/lens catalog

Reviewed 2026-11-17. Scope: mainland China launches from 2022 through the review date; at least one launch configuration strictly above CNY 4000. This includes higher-capacity configurations of models whose entry price was below CNY 4000. No current promotional price is used as launch MSRP.

Optical values and hardware model codes come from OPPO specification pages. Price evidence uses manufacturer launch announcements when available, otherwise contemporary first-sale/launch reporting (explicitly tagged in the data). These price reports are eligibility evidence, not technical lens specifications.

FOV without a documented axis is retained as manufacturerFov with fovAxis: unspecified and never converted to equivalent focal length. Reno9 Pro+ and Reno10 Pro+ lack some verified native equivalents and therefore require manual lens setup. Danxia color sensors are not image cameras and are omitted; macro sensors without a verified aperture are omitted. No portrait crop or digital zoom is listed as a physical camera.

Only hardware codes shown by the cited model references participate in automatic matching. Satellite-edition codes PMA120 and PKH120 were excluded because the listed specification pages do not document them; users can still select a reviewed phone and enter or confirm their lens parameters manually.

Reno11 Pro is excluded: its actual launch configurations were CNY 3499/3799; a CNY 4199 pre-release rumor does not qualify. Reno14 and Reno15 standard versions also do not exceed CNY 4000 at launch.
